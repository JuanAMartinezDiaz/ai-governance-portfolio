An automated review is not independent human endorsement. The record should be updated after Juan actually reviews the assumptions, challenges the decisions, changes any wording and explains the tests. A dated note should capture his agreement or disagreement and the artifact revision; it should not be backdated or inferred from permission to publish.

All organizations, vendors, candidates, applicants, approvals and incident events in the case studies are fictional. Local scripts process synthetic fixtures. Proposed control tests and supplier evidence remain distinct from executed checks. No employer/customer material, real applicant data or fabricated signature is included.

The portfolio is intended to make reasoning inspectable. Reviewers can follow a business decision to its source, control, fixture, result and remaining limitation through [TRACEABILITY.md](TRACEABILITY.md), then use the [interview walkthrough](INTERVIEW.md) to challenge the reasoning.
