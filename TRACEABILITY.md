## Read the evidence states correctly

**Executed local checks** establish behavior of the supplied scripts and fixture arithmetic at a particular revision. **Constructed inputs** are invented case facts and outputs. **Designed controls** specify what still needs implementation or testing. **Human/operational assurance** requires actual reviewers, relevant data, a deployed boundary and sufficient coverage. The [verification record](VERIFICATION.md) identifies what ran; [authorship](AUTHORSHIP.md) identifies the human-review limit.

The C6 correction is deliberate: blocking tool actions and leakage alone would miss an instruction that silently changed a shortlist. The full planned assessment must examine ranking and criterion manipulation. The local extraction demonstration instead forbids ranking entirely and compares supported fields to trusted fixture facts. Passing that narrower contract does not establish that a generative model is immune to prompt injection.
