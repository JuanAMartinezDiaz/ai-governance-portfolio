# Worked challenge and exception disposition

**EX-003-01 · fictional role-based decision record · 12 November 2026.** This is a completed writing exercise. No actual executive, lawyer or committee met, signed, or granted authority.

| Record | Scenario position | Evidence assessed |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Worked disposition

**Reject the requested live-use exception.** Retain the manual process. Record the unresolved risk objection against DEC-003 and assign HR the evidence and business-case follow-up. Neither a 30-day expiry nor commercial urgency supplies the missing control evidence.

**Allow the next decision to concern an isolated synthetic exercise.** The bounded `SYN-EXTRACT-001` demonstration contains invented records and proposed outputs, no ranking or applicant action, and no real data or external model. Its [generated evidence](../evidence/control-lifecycle.md) can inform a subsequent review of a narrower extraction alternative. Its `SIMULATED_LIMITED_APPROVAL` state is a program result, not a policy exception, human signoff, legal clearance, or permission to deploy CandidateRank.

| Follow-up | Accountable scenario role | Closure condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Compare manual, ranking and extraction options | HR executive | [Business case](../business-case/README.md) assumptions challenged; actual pilot measurements required before benefits are claimed |
| Resolve C1–C8 findings | Respective control owners | Independent evaluation of relevant evidence; unresolved objections retained |
| Reassess narrower purpose | Business owner with Legal and risk | Scope, downstream influence, data and legal applicability established |
| Seek a live release decision | Authorized business/committee role | Identity, delegation, exact version, conditions and evidence recorded; absent here |

A future exception that is eligible under policy must specify the exact requirement, compensating control, owner, expiry and withdrawal trigger. The person requesting it cannot close an independent finding or impersonate the approver. Expiry, a material change or loss of a required control returns the case to hold.
