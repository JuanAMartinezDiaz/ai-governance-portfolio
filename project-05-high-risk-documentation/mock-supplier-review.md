# Mock supplier response and evidence adjudication

**RFI-011 · synthetic diligence exercise · 12 November 2026.** All supplier statements below are invented assessment inputs. No actual vendor responded, furnished a technical file or passed validation. HarborBridge remains the assumed deployer; role allocation requires confirmation.

| Request / mock response | Independent assessment | Disposition and required evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intended use: “Ranks candidates; HR makes the final choice.” | Human final selection does not resolve ranking influence or change the stated employment use | Retain proposed high-risk classification; obtain versioned purpose, limits and user instructions |
| Quality: “95% accurate on our internal benchmark.” | Metric, population, task, denominator and error burden are undefined | Reject as release evidence; request evaluation protocol, job relevance, subgroup uncertainty and reproducible results |
| Data: “Customer information is secure.” | No data-flow, retention, training-use or subprocessor detail | Request scoped data-flow and contractual evidence; no real CV access |
| Change: “We regularly improve the model.” | Silent updates could invalidate local assessment | Require identified versions, notice, revalidation criteria and rollback arrangements |
| Oversight: “Users can override.” | Capability, permissions, time and evidence for effective challenge are absent | Require interface inspection and observed reviewer exercises, including omitted applicants |
| Logs: “An audit report is available.” | No decision correlation, completeness, access or integrity evidence | Request sample schema and retrieval/alteration tests; a report title alone is insufficient |
| Incident support: “Support responds promptly.” | No named escalation mechanism or demonstrated unavailable-provider procedure | Request contact path, responsibilities, response commitments and a joint exercise |
| Conformity: “We comply with all AI rules.” | No applicable declaration, technical-document index or documented conformity route supplied | Request the provider's supporting package; do not equate a statement with conformity |

## What changes the decision?

Procurement owns follow-up; Legal assesses roles, duties and contracts; validation tests relevant evidence; technology demonstrates local controls; HR owns the use and business case. Each response receives a version, reviewer, finding, due date and evidence link. A supplier cannot close its own independent finding.

**DEC-011 remains DO NOT DEPLOY.** The responses above help specify the diligence request but clear no release gate. The standalone recruitment example requires the applicable provider evidence and local controls described in the [readiness index](conformity-readiness.md). If evidence remains inadequate at the proposed diligence deadline, retain the manual process and compare another supplier or a narrower use.

The [bounded extraction demonstration](../evidence/control-lifecycle.md) tests a locally written contract against invented output proposals. It does not validate this fictional supplier or its absent model. Its findings can inform control requirements, while its result cannot substitute for missing provider evidence.
